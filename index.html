What you'll hit

Bomb — huge forward blast
Spring — launches you skyward
Balloon — strong lift
Spikes — kills your momentum
Bird — knocks you down
Plane — land on top to launch,
clip the underside and you drop
UFO — jackpot, needs real height

Power-ups

Shield — absorbs one hazard hit
Wind — one big blast forward
Low gravity — 3s of huge bounces

They float between the birds and the UFOs. You carry one at a time and set it off yourself — click while airborne.

Chain assists without touching the ground and each one hits harder, up to 4×.

Controls

Click / Space — set angle, then power
Click / Space in flight — use power-up
Click / Space — launch again after landing
R — new world

Only shown with ?dev. Fixed 40° launch at standard power, just short of the boundary.
